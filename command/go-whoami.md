Call the `go_whoami` tool (verbose) and report:
- provider (`opencode-go` vs `opencode`)
- masked key + fingerprint
- email label from `~/.config/opencode/go-accounts.json` (or "unlabelled" with instructions to label it)
- reminder that https://opencode.ai/auth is the source of truth for email
